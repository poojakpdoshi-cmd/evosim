Core Concept

A Living Digital Terrarium

🌿 The Ecosystem Sandbox

Instead of scripting dumb bots with rigid if-else rules, EvoSim creates a miniature physics world with living stakes:

  • Organisms swim through a 2D aquatic arena.
  • Every stroke of motion consumes biological energy.
  • Eating food replenishes energy and unlocks reproduction.
  • Energy hits zero? Death and permanent removal from the gene pool.

🧠 100% Unprogrammed Intelligence

Nobody teaches them how to eat.

Generation 1 is born with totally randomized synaptic connections. Some spin in chaotic circles, others crash into perimeter walls. Survival is purely earned through generation-by-generation genetic mutation.

✨ Complex strategies like predator evasion and energy-conserving gliding emerge spontaneously!

Technical Architecture

Anatomy of an Organism: Sensors, Brain & Motors

👁️ 1. Sensory Eyes

  • 5-7 directional raycast whiskers
  • Nearest food distance & angle
  • Wall & obstacle proximity
  • Internal metabolic energy gauge

🧬 2. Neural Micro-Brain

  • Feed-Forward Neural Network
  • Input: 6 sensory readings
  • Hidden: 8 Tanh neurons
  • Output: 2 motor controls
  • DNA: Synaptic weight matrix

⚡ 3. Motor Muscles

  • Steering angle: Turn left / right
  • Forward thrust: Speed burst
  • Inertia, momentum & water drag
  • High speed = faster energy burn
Evolutionary Progression

From Pure Chaos to Super-Organisms

MIN 0:00

Gen 1: Chaos

Random brain weights. Organisms spin in place, slam walls, and 95% quickly starve.

MIN 2:30

Gen 10: Luck

A few creatures accidentally swim forward, bump into food, and pass on mutated genes.

MIN 6:00

Gen 30: Hunters

Sensors are now wired to steering. Creatures spot food, rotate directly toward it, and charge.

MIN 12:00+

Gen 60: Masters

Energy conservation: Gliding at low energy, sharp braking at food, and avoiding dead-ends.
